Dailies — design directions

Five takes on the same idea: six small things, every day, stored only on your device and reset at midnight. Tap a card to open it. They share state, so ticking something in one shows up in all of them.

1 · Canopy

Closest to the inspiration. Grainy sky over pines, chalk icons. The sky clears and warms as you finish.

2 · Field Notes

A page from a pocket notebook. Ink checkboxes, pen strike-throughs, tally marks for progress.

3 · Daylight

The sky follows the real time of day. The sun travels its arc as you complete things.

4 · Patches

Trail-badge aesthetic. Each daily is an embroidered patch you stitch on when it's done.

5 · Bold

Type only. Outlined words fill in solid as you go, with a big score up top.
